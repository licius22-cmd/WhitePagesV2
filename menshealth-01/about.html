About & disclosure

Everyday Men’s Guide is an educational website that explains men’s health and erectile dysfunction in plain language for adults in the United States.

What this guide covers

The guide introduces general health concepts, everyday habits that public health agencies describe as part of a healthy routine, and questions to consider before using a dietary supplement. It is meant to support informed conversations with qualified healthcare professionals, not to replace them.

How content is prepared

Health statements in this guide are based on publicly available information from U.S. government health agencies and professional medical organizations. Each statement is cited and linked to its source on the main page, and content is updated when those sources change. This guide is not a healthcare provider or an independent medical review.

Commercial disclosure

This guide is published in a supplement-marketing context. The publisher may have commercial relationships with companies that market dietary supplements and may receive compensation related to those products. These relationships do not change the information presented here: the guide does not evaluate or recommend a particular product, and it does not claim that any supplement diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any disease.

Medical disclaimer

Content is for general educational purposes only and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Individual health needs vary. Questions about a health condition, medication, or supplement should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional. In a medical emergency, call 911.

Third-party links

Links to government agencies and professional organizations are provided for reference. They do not imply that those organizations endorse this website or any product.
