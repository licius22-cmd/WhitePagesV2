Baking soda and weight loss

What to know about sodium bicarbonate and the weight-loss claims that circulate online.

Drinking baking soda is not a proven way to lose weight. Using it as a home remedy can cause harm.

What the evidence says

Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate, a common kitchen ingredient. Its ability to react with acids does not establish that drinking it reduces body fat. Weight-loss claims about baking soda lack reliable clinical evidence.

Why safety matters

Swallowing too much baking soda can lead to excess sodium and disrupt the body’s acid-base balance. It can also release gas in the stomach. Poison Control and MedlinePlus explain these risks and why misuse can require medical attention.

How to assess a weight-loss claim

  • Look for research in people that directly measures the claimed outcome.
  • Check whether a source describes risks and limitations alongside any benefits.
  • Discuss personal questions about weight management with a qualified health professional.
